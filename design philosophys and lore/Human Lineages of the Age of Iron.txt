Human Lineages of the Age of Iron
Designer's Note: The player's true self is the Wretched Blade — the sword. Lineage describes the Projected Body: the humanoid avatar the blade generates to interact with the physical world. Lineage is chosen at the start of a run and shapes that avatar's passive stat profile. The sword itself is unchanged; only the body it projects is different.

In the grim aftermath of seven Hexocausts, humanity in the Severed Lands has endured, mutated, and adapted. No longer a monolithic group, the human species has fractured into distinct lineages, each forged by the unique environmental scars and Nullpulse distortions of their ancestral lands. These are not just cultures; they are true sub-races, bearing unique visual characteristics, inherent strengths, and profound weaknesses. They are the Cinders, Hollows, Glass, Marrow, and Dross, each a testament to humanity's grim perseverance and a vital force in the brutal Age of Iron.
All humans share the fundamental drives of ambition, resourcefulness, and a burning will to survive and dominate, making them formidable contenders against the Nullmen and each other.
________________


Cinders
The Cinders are the descendants of those who endured the initial Hexocausts in the most directly affected urban and industrial zones, where Geocrash Hexes fractured the very ground beneath them. Cinder lineage is defined by hardened resilience and a stark, unyielding nature, having literally risen from the ash and rubble.
* Visual Parallel: Cinders are primarily represented by traits associated with White/Caucasian individuals. Their appearance is often characterized by a lean, sinewy build, sallow or pale complexion with visible, ropy veins, sharp and severe features, and deep-set eyes that hold an ancient weariness. They favor practical, durable clothing, often patched with salvaged metals and reinforced fabrics, moving with an efficient, grounded gait.
* Innate Bonus: Inertial Fortitude. Cinders possess an inherent resistance to physical displacement and blunt force trauma. They take reduced damage from physical impacts, are less likely to be staggered by powerful blows, and recover from being knocked down with surprising speed. Their bodies are dense, almost like living anchors against the chaos.
* Drawback: Muted Insight. Generations spent in a brutally physical reality have subtly dulled their sensory and psychic awareness. They struggle to perceive subtle Nullpulse disturbances, making them more susceptible to abstract, non-physical Hexes (like Echoscream or Memoreave), which can disorient them more easily. They also find it slightly harder to perceive environmental cues related to hidden Essence.
* Cultural Identity: Cinders often form tight-knit, disciplined communities, valuing pragmatism, self-sufficiency, and a grim determination to endure. They are the backbone of many Last Bastions, known for their stoicism, their proficiency in manual labor, and their unwavering defense of their territories.

Hollows
The Hollows were forged in the bleak, light-devouring regions scarred by Voidrend Hexes (which distort existence and create hollow, consuming spaces). They have adapted to thrive where others simply cease to be, developing a strange, unsettling connection to the Nullpulse's destructive, consuming aspects, as if something fundamental has been scooped out of them, leaving behind a chilling efficiency.
* Visual Parallel: Hollows are primarily represented by traits associated with Asian individuals. Their appearance is striking and often unsettling: unnaturally pale or even translucent skin with faint, visible veins of darker, almost void-like energy pulsing beneath the surface. Their eyes are frequently devoid of conventional irises, appearing as pure, dark obsidian, or sometimes glowing with a faint, cold light. Their forms are wiry and gaunt, yet capable of surprising speed and agility, as if unburdened by conventional mass. Their clothing is typically minimal and practical, often made from mutated animal hides or plant fibers, favoring quick, unhindered movement that blends seamlessly with the desolate environment.
* Innate Bonus: Essence Scavenge. Hollows are exceptionally efficient at drawing power from the Nullpulse directly from the fallen. They gain a significant bonus to Essence collected from enemies. Additionally, when they defeat an enemy with a Hex, they have an increased chance to immediately regain a portion of that Hex's Essence cost, allowing for more aggressive and sustained use of abilities.
* Drawback: Existential Fragility. Their deep, almost parasitic, connection to the Nullpulse's volatile energies makes their physical forms inherently less stable. They have naturally lower maximum health and are more vulnerable to environmental hazards and large-scale area-of-effect damage, as if their very existence is less anchored to reality. They also experience a slightly reduced resistance to "void-like" Nullpulse effects that distort matter or space.
* Cultural Identity: Hollows are often nomadic and highly adaptive, functioning as master scavengers, expert hunters, and efficient raiders. They are pragmatic and often ruthless, valuing immediate gain, self-reliance, and individual survival above all else. They are feared for their chilling efficiency and their ability to thrive in the most desolate and dangerous zones, often forming fluid, opportunistic bands.

Glass
The Glass lineages emerged from communities that survived within the most fragile and warped zones, where the very air felt brittle from prolonged exposure to distorted Nullpulse frequencies, perhaps from subtle Hex interactions that left the landscape transparent but precarious. They have adapted a unique clarity of perception, but at a cost to their physical robustness.
* Visual Parallel: Glass are primarily represented by traits associated with Middle Eastern and North African (MENA) individuals. Their appearance is slender and almost delicate, with unnaturally smooth and often pale skin, sometimes with a faint, almost translucent quality. Their eyes are strikingly clear, wide, and appear highly perceptive, often seeming to shimmer like refracted light. They move with precise, almost crystalline grace. Their attire tends to be light, layered, and often incorporates reflective or subtly shimmering materials.
* Innate Bonus: Precise Focus. Glass possess an innate clarity of thought and heightened sensory acuity. This grants them a bonus to critical hit chance and critical damage with precision-based attacks. They also have a natural resistance to disorientation and visual impairment, seeing through most forms of Nullpulse-induced haze or illusions. They possess a keen eye for details, slightly improving their chances of finding hidden Essence.
* Drawback: Brittle Form. Their physical bodies are inherently more delicate. Glass have lower base health and are more susceptible to sustained physical damage and area-of-effect attacks. They also take increased damage from direct, concentrated Hex attacks.
* Cultural Identity: Glass societies are often small, insular, and highly intellectual, valuing clarity of thought, precise execution, and the pursuit of truth even in a fractured world. They serve as strategists, elite marksmen, specialized artisans of intricate mechanisms, and are often sought for their unparalleled ability to decipher complex problems or identify weaknesses.

Marrow
The Marrow lineages are born from the deepest, most resilient pockets of humanity, enduring the pervasive decay and subtle corruption of the Nullpulse by adapting at a fundamental, biological level. They have an almost primal connection to the very "marrow" or essence of life in the Severed Lands, able to draw sustenance and resilience where others cannot.
* Visual Parallel: Marrow are primarily represented by traits associated with Black/African individuals. They often have a robust, almost primal physical presence, with skin that can appear coarse, earthy, or subtly mottled, reflecting a deep integration with the mutated biomass of the world. Their features are often strong and unyielding. They might display minor, subtle biological adaptations, such as unusually strong fingernails or slightly tougher skin patches. Their attire is often practical and organic, using materials harvested from resilient flora and fauna.
* Innate Bonus: Adaptive Vigor. Marrow possess exceptional biological resilience and regenerative capabilities. They gain a bonus to maximum health and have improved resistance to status effects (like poison, bleed, or Hexic corruption over time). Their connection to the Nullpulse's life-giving (or twisting) forces allows them to gain a small amount of Essence back from any source of healing.
* Drawback: Primal Draw. Their deep, fundamental connection makes them subtly susceptible to the raw, uncontrolled power of the Nullpulse. They may experience occasional, minor debuffs or visual distortions from strong ambient Nullpulse fields, and their attacks are less precise, suffering a slight penalty to critical hit chance.
* Cultural Identity: Marrow communities are often focused on self-sufficiency, resource management, and enduring the unpredictable nature of the Severed Lands through sheer biological will. They are formidable survivalists, often excelling in crafting, foraging, and close-quarters combat, forming the backbone of resilient pioneer or scavenger groups.

Dross
The Dross lineage traces its origins to the absolute fringes of civilization, the desperate, resourceful survivors who learned to thrive amidst the accumulated waste, decay, and forgotten corners of the world. They are the epitome of resourcefulness, finding value and life where others see only ruin.
* Visual Parallel: Dross are primarily represented by traits associated with Latino/Hispanic individuals. They often appear gaunt or wiry, with skin that may be stained with the grime of industry or the ash of ruins. Their features are sharp, often with quick, darting eyes that miss nothing. They move with an agile, almost furtive grace, always aware of their surroundings. Their clothing is typically a patchwork of salvaged materials, expertly mended and adapted for stealth and utility, often layered to blend into the desolate landscape.
* Innate Bonus: Scavenger's Cunning. Dross possess an unparalleled knack for finding and utilizing resources. They gain a significant bonus to the amount of Essence found from all sources (enemy kills, environmental finds). They also have a natural affinity for avoiding detection, gaining a slight bonus to stealth and evasion.
* Drawback: Social Isolation. Generations of living on the fringes have made them less trusting and less capable of forming strong social bonds. They suffer a minor penalty to any cooperative or faction-based mechanics, and their starting reputation with established groups may be slightly lower. Their physical combat prowess is also slightly diminished due to their focus on evasion and scavenging.
* Cultural Identity: Dross typically form loose, opportunistic bands or operate as solitary figures. They are master scavengers, infiltrators, and information brokers, valuing freedom, ingenuity, and a ruthless pragmatism. They exist in the shadows of the larger factions, often moving between territories, exploiting opportunities and remaining fiercely independent.
