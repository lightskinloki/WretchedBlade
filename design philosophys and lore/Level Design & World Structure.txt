Level Design & World Structure
In the Severed Lands, every region is a statement. The world is not a connected, seamless journey but rather a collection of contested territories, each ruled by a Dominator and defined by the unique Hex that scarred it. The player's progression through these regions is non-linear, driven by the Wretched Blade's hunt for the seven Dominators and the Tuning Forks they guard.

________________


The Seven Dominators & Their Regions
The Severed Lands are divided into seven distinct regions, each corresponding to one of the seven Hexes that have ravaged the world. Each region is under the dominion of a single Dominator — an ancient, powerful Nullman who embodies that region's Hex and has spent eons accumulating power and territory. Defeating a Dominator weakens their grip on the region, shifting the balance of power and opening paths previously blocked by their influence.

* Dominator 1: The Shattered Sovereign (Geocrash Hex)
  * Region: The Fractured Yards
  * Aesthetic: A landscape of cubic, angular ruins. Massive concrete and steel structures hang at unnatural angles, held by Hex-warped gravity. Platforms are jagged and unstable; the earth itself is fractured into columns and ravines. Color palette: grays, rust, dusty browns.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Geocrash-based attacks. Enemies use lesser versions: basic ground fractures, rubble throws, destabilization effects.
  * Mini-Bosses: 2-3 territorial lieutenants (Geocrash-aligned) guard sub-regions within the Yards.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Stability — grants the Resonance Art "Earthward," a grounding ability that increases poise and reduces knockback.

* Dominator 2: The Void Echo (Voidrend Hex)
  * Region: The Hollow Expanse
  * Aesthetic: A landscape where reality has been "scooped out." Jagged void-edges, floating islands of solid matter separated by empty space. The air feels thin and distorted. Gravity is unreliable. Color palette: blacks, deep purples, sickly greens, faint void-light.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Voidrend-based attacks. Enemies use lesser versions: small existence distortions, localized voids, reality tears.
  * Mini-Bosses: 2-3 creatures born from the Voidrend itself, protective entities that exist at the boundaries of reality.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Severance — grants the Resonance Art "Untether," allowing brief periods of gravity-defiant movement.

* Dominator 3: The Screaming Spire (Echoscream Hex)
  * Region: The Resonant Ruins
  * Aesthetic: A landscape of warped, impossibly tall towers that seem to warp sound itself. Walls are translucent and glitch-like; sound distorts vision. The environment actively disorients. Color palette: silvers, pale blues, whites with audiovisual glitch artifacts.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Echoscream-based attacks. Enemies use lesser versions: sound-based stuns, disorientation waves, auditory attacks that cause visual distortion.
  * Mini-Bosses: 2-3 resonance-aligned entities, possibly humanoid forms trapped within the towers.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Clarity — grants the Resonance Art "Silence," which dampens environmental distortion and provides brief periods of focused perception.

* Dominator 4: The Memory Thief (Memoreave Hex)
  * Region: The Forgotten Archive
  * Aesthetic: A landscape of libraries, monuments, and memory-warped structures. Reality "glitches" and shifts based on memory corruption. The same room can appear different depending on perspective. Visuals include distorted architecture, temporal echoes, and fragmented imagery. Color palette: faded sepia, washed grays, ghostly whites.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Memoreave-based attacks. Enemies use lesser versions: memory corruption (disabling abilities temporarily), temporal distortion (slowing or fast-forwarding the player), identity confusion (swapping controls).
  * Mini-Bosses: 2-3 entities bound to specific memories, possibly echoes of past civilizations.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Continuity — grants the Resonance Art "Anchor," which provides temporary immunity to temporal/memory distortion.

* Dominator 5: The Nullpulse Heart (Nullpulse Hex)
  * Region: The Unraveling Core
  * Aesthetic: A landscape where the Nullpulse itself is visibly unraveling. The ground is unstable and fragmenting. Energy crackles at the edges of reality. The world feels like it's actively collapsing. Color palette: violent purples, blacks, electric blues, sickly greens.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Raw Nullpulse instability. Enemies use lesser versions: spontaneous environmental collapse, raw energy blasts, Essence drains.
  * Mini-Bosses: 2-3 creatures that have become one with the Nullpulse, barely maintaining form.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Continuity — grants the Resonance Art "Endure," which provides brief resistance to environmental collapse.

* Dominator 6: The Rust Tyrant (Technomantic Corruption)
  * Region: The Corroded Expanse
  * Aesthetic: A landscape of rusted machinery, decaying technology, and bio-mechanical corruption. The remains of a once-technological civilization, now overtaken by Nullpulse influence. Environments are a mixture of industrial decay and organic growth. Color palette: deep oranges, rust reds, sickly greens, blacks.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Technomantic-derived attacks. Enemies use lesser versions: corrosion fields, mechanical projectiles, bio-technological blasts.
  * Mini-Bosses: 2-3 hybrid entities — part machine, part Nullman, part corrupted human.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Adaptation — grants the Resonance Art "Transmute," allowing brief defensive form-shifts.

* Dominator 7: The Final Echo (The Wretched Blade's Inverse)
  * Region: The Throne of Ashes
  * Aesthetic: The heart of the Severed Lands, where the Wretched Blade's power is strongest and weakest. A landscape of ash, bone, and the echoes of all prior Hexocausts. Visually it mirrors and distorts all previous regions. Color palette: monochromatic grays, deep blacks, and stark whites.
  * Thematic Hex Family: Composite — draws from all seven Hexes, reflecting the Wretched Blade's unique origin.
  * Mini-Bosses: None — this region is the final confrontation.
  * Tuning Fork Reward: The Fork of Origin — grants access to the "Grand Resonance," the ultimate Resonance Art.

Room Types & Flow
The Severed Lands are divided into discrete "rooms" — self-contained 2D side-scrolling spaces connected by exits. Each room is one of several types, and their arrangement creates a natural flow and pacing structure.

* Combat Rooms: Arenas with 1-4 mid-tier enemies (Raiders, Enforcers, Stalkers). These rooms are heavily populated and demand active combat. The room is clear only when all enemies are defeated.
  * Exits: Combat rooms typically lead to Exploration or Transition rooms, allowing players to progress after a challenge.
  * Essence Reward: Enemies drop Essence — the primary resource for healing, buying items, and unlocking Lesser Hexes.
  * Difficulty: Moderate. Designed to be surmountable by an attentive player with basic equipment.

* Exploration Rooms: Sparsely populated or empty spaces with environmental hazards, secret alcoves, and resource nodes (Essence Pores, Weapon Form Fragments, item caches). These rooms reward careful attention and risk-taking.
  * Exits: Exploration rooms typically branch into multiple paths, providing non-linear choice. Some exits are hidden or require specific actions to unlock.
  * Lore Reward: Environmental storytelling — fallen structures, corpses, inscriptions, and subtle visual clues hinting at the region's history and the Dominator's influence.
  * Difficulty: Low to moderate. Environmental hazards (spikes, pits, falling debris) are the primary threat, not enemy density.

* Transition Rooms: Visually distinct "interlude" spaces between combat-heavy and exploration-heavy areas. These rooms establish atmosphere and provide narrative breathing room.
  * Exits: Usually linear, leading to a specific next room (either a boss arena or a major exploration branch).
  * Hazard Reward: May contain a single resource node or a minor enemy encounter.
  * Difficulty: Very low. These are pacing tools, not difficulty gates.

* Boss Arenas: Large, open chambers reserved for Mini-World Bosses and Dominators. These rooms are visually distinct and architecturally designed to showcase the boss's power.
  * Exits: Typically one entry and one exit (locked until the boss is defeated). Some boss arenas may have a Tuning Fork checkpoint at the exit.
  * Boss Reward: Defeating a Dominator grants the player a significant Essence reward and access to a Tuning Fork (and thus a Resonance Art).
  * Difficulty: High. These are skill-check moments, requiring mastery of combat mechanics.

* Safe Rooms (Sanctuaries): Checkpoints with a Tuning Fork and no enemies. These rooms are small, visually calm, and serve as respawn points. The player can rest here, attune to a different Resonance Art, and prepare for the next challenge.
  * Exits: Sanctuaries are typically placed before major boss arenas or at the start of new regions.
  * Lore Reward: May contain cryptic NPCs (Custodians) offering hints about the region or the Dominators.
  * Difficulty: None. Pure safe space.

Non-Linear Progression Structure
The Severed Lands are not a linear hallway. Instead, they form a loosely interconnected web of regions and rooms, allowing players to approach Dominators in any order. The world is designed with natural "gates" that prevent sequence-breaking while still allowing freedom of choice.

* Region Entry Points: Each region is accessed via a specific Entry Room (often a Transition Room at the border). To reach a region, the player must navigate to its entry from another region or from the central Hub.

* The Hub (Optional): A central safe room that connects to all seven regions. This serves as a natural waypoint and allows players to choose their path. (Alternatively, regions can be accessed through environmental connections without a central hub, creating a more organic web structure.)

* Escape Valves: If a player encounters a Dominator and is underpowered, they can retreat and explore other regions, gathering Essence, new Weapon Forms, and Resonance Arts before returning stronger. This prevents the game from feeling punitive.

* Gating Mechanics (Subtle): Some exits are blocked by environmental conditions (e.g., a Geocrash-scarred gap that requires the "Stability" Resonance Art to cross) or by Dominator-aligned enemies who only retreat after the Dominator is defeated. This creates a natural progression incentive without hard locks.

Pacing & Density
Each region contains approximately 8-12 rooms, divided roughly as:
* 3-4 Combat Rooms
* 3-4 Exploration Rooms
* 1-2 Transition Rooms
* 1 Boss Arena
* 1 Sanctuary (checkpoint)

This ensures a rhythm of: Combat → Exploration → Transition → Boss, with Safe Rooms placed strategically to prevent frustration.

Within each room:
* Combat Rooms: ~1 enemy per 100 pixels of horizontal space (1-4 enemies in typical ~400px wide room)
* Exploration Rooms: ~0-1 enemy per room, heavy environmental hazard emphasis
* Boss Arenas: 1 single, powerful foe with distinct attack patterns and visual telegraphs

Visual & Atmospheric Progression
Each region should feel visually distinct while maintaining the overall grim, desolate aesthetic. The progression through regions should feel like a descent into deeper, more corrupted layers of the Severed Lands:

1. The Fractured Yards (Geocrash): Industrial decay, cubist geometry, rust and concrete.
2. The Hollow Expanse (Voidrend): Unsettling, reality-bending, sparse and isolating.
3. The Resonant Ruins (Echoscream): Glitch-like, tower-heavy, audiovisual distortion.
4. The Forgotten Archive (Memoreave): Fractured temporality, library aesthetics, ghostly echoes.
5. The Unraveling Core (Nullpulse): Raw, chaotic, violently collapsing.
6. The Corroded Expanse (Technomantic): Bio-mechanical fusion, rust and organic growth.
7. The Throne of Ashes (Composite): A mirror-world reflecting all previous regions, culminating in starkness.
