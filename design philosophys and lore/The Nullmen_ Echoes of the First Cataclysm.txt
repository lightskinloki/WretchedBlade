The Nullmen: Echoes of the First Cataclysm
Nullmen are not human, nor are they a simple mutation. They are a distinct and formidable species, born from the most catastrophic event in the world's history: the First Hexocaust. Their very existence is a chilling testament to humanity's ambition and the Nullpulse's devastating power.
Origin: The Collision of Consciousness and Chaos
Nullmen are the terrifying and unforeseen consequence of the human consciousness field violently colliding with the Nullpulse field during the unprecedented energies of the First Hexocaust. In that moment of interdimensional annihilation, countless human egos – their will, their desires, their very sentience – were not simply destroyed. Instead, they were preserved as indelible, warped imprints directly onto the Nullpulse itself. They are, quite literally, the metaphysical "impact craters" of human consciousness on the Nullpulse.
* Ancient Genesis, Later Emergence: While Nullmen were forged in the cataclysm of the First Hexocaust, they did not begin to widely reform and appear in the Severed Lands until the 4th Age. This prolonged, unseen gestation period within the Nullpulse preceded their widespread physical manifestation as a dominant force.
* Innate Nullpulse Connection: All Nullmen possess an inherent, deep connection to the Nullpulse. They draw upon its raw energy, Essence, for their very existence, sustenance, and, in many cases, to manifest Hexic abilities.
* Cyclical Immortality: Nullmen cannot truly be "killed" by other Nullmen. When defeated, their forms dissipate, but their imprinted essence within the Nullpulse eventually reforms. This is why Dominators are locked in perpetual, unwinnable wars for territory and power; they can only temporarily vanquish rivals.

Unique Forms: Manifestations of Warped Ego
The vast diversity in Nullmen forms stems directly from their origin as imprinted consciousness. Their appearances often reflect the unique individual egos, ambitions, and fears that were seared onto the Nullpulse.
* From Instinct to Intellect: Nullmen span a wide spectrum of intelligence and sentience. Some are instinct-driven, beast-like creatures, while others, particularly the Dominators and their Lieutenants, retain formidable intellects, strategic cunning, and complex motivations that are terrifying reflections of their human predecessors.
* Physical Manifestations: Their forms are diverse, ranging from gaunt, ethereal figures to hulking, grotesque giants. Each manifestation is a distorted mirror of the specific "frequency" or chaos of the Nullpulse, and the imprinted ego, that gave them birth. Some may retain vague humanoid outlines, while others are utterly alien.

The Wretched Blade: A Primordial Scar on the Soul
The player character, The Wretched Blade, is a unique entity among Nullmen, a reverse-formed Nullman.
* The Cataclysmic Inversion: While other Nullmen result from human consciousness colliding with the Nullpulse, The Wretched Blade is the terrifying outcome of the Nullpulse itself violently colliding with the collective human consciousness field during the First Hexocaust. It is the impact crater of the Nullpulse on the very fabric of the human subconscious.
* Delayed Formation: This profound inversion explains why The Wretched Blade took significantly longer to form and physically manifest in the Severed Lands compared to other Nullmen. It was the Nullpulse carving a foundational wound, an 'anti-ego,' within the collective human psyche, slowly solidifying into a physical embodiment of that cosmic scar.
* Explaining Unique Traits:
   * Primordial Nature: The Wretched Blade wasn't just present at the First Hexocaust; it was forged by the fundamental shockwave that defined that event.
   * The "Innate Hex": It is the primordial Hex, a foundational rupture. This explains its ability to transmute into other Weapon Forms (it inherently understands the Nullpulse's underlying chaos) and its power to permakill other Nullmen (it represents the fundamental erasure of the Nullpulse, capable of annihilating even the imprinted egos of others).
   * Projected Body: Its humanoid body is merely an energetic projection, an avatar generated by The Wretched Blade's will to interact with the physical world. This highlights its deep, unique connection to both the Nullpulse (from which it is made) and the human consciousness field (from which it was scarred). In the 2D world, this projection is the player's visible sprite — the sword is the soul, the body is the instrument.
   * 2D Encounter Range: Nullmen manifest across a wide spectrum of side-scrolling encounters — from instinct-driven beasts that patrol corridors to Dominator boss fights that dominate entire room boundaries, their scale deliberately dwarfing the Projected Body to communicate hierarchy and threat.
   * Unique Attunement (Resonance Arts): Its origin as an "impact" on the consciousness field grants it an unparalleled sensitivity to the Nullpulse's deeper, natural frequencies.

Relationship with Other Races: A World of Converging Ambitions
The Severed Lands are a brutal tapestry woven from the ambitions and struggles of multiple formidable races.
* Humans: Resilient and Dangerous Survivors

   * Humans are numerous, resilient, and highly dangerous in the Severed Lands, not fragile or endangered. They live alongside and directly compete with Nullmen.
   * They stand on equal footing in terms of overall presence and power. The Severed Lands are mostly inhabited by Nullmen in the same way a region might be mostly desert; it's just their natural environment.
   * Driven by Mortality: Unlike Nullmen, humans face permanent mortality. This fuels a stronger, more urgent drive for power, luxury, and dominance, making them incredibly ambitious, ruthless, and willing to take extreme risks. Their ambition manifests in technological innovation, tactical cunning, and unyielding will.
   * Interactions: Conflict for resources and territory is common. Uneasy truces or exploitative relationships can form, where technology is traded for power, but always with deep suspicion. Both races are capable of subjugating the other. Desperate human cults may even worship or serve powerful Nullmen.
   * Mutated Flora and Fauna: The World's Savage Life

      * The Nullpulse's effects extend beyond sentient races, creating a wide array of dangerous, mutated plants and creatures that fill the ecological niches of the Severed Lands.
      * These creatures serve as prey, apex predators, or environmental hazards for both Nullmen and humans. Both races may attempt to control or domesticate them.
      * The Wretched Blade (Player Nullman):

         * Nullmen Perception: Other Nullmen, particularly Dominators, recognize The Wretched Blade as a Nullman, but one of unprecedented power and an existential threat. They instinctively sense its unique ability to truly permakill them, making it their ultimate, terrifying enemy.
         * Human Perception: Humans perceive The Wretched Blade (and its projected body) as another terrifying Nullman, a potential harbinger of doom, or a desperate chance at salvation, depending on the player's actions and chosen path (Hex Affinity).
