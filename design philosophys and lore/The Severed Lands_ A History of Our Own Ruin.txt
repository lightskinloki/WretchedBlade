The Severed Lands: A History of Our Own Ruin
The history of the world is a cyclical tale of humanity's ambition, culminating in self-inflicted cataclysm and a twisted evolution, all centered around the manipulation and perversion of a fundamental cosmic force.
________________


The Primordial Age (Our World, Its History)
This era is, in essence, our current world and its history. Humanity, driven by its endless pursuit of knowledge and power, made scientific and technological advancements, leading to discoveries that mirrored profound cosmic truths.
* Discovery of Resonance (Proto-Physics/Cosmology): Humanity gained a sophisticated understanding of the universe's fundamental forces. This wasn't about conventional physics; it was about discerning the natural, underlying frequencies within the Nullpulse's composition – the very fabric of reality itself. This knowledge allowed for a deeper, more harmonious (or at least, less destructive) interaction with existence.
* Creation of Tuning Forks (Ancient Technology/Art): Through this profound, almost spiritual, understanding, the Tuning Forks were developed. These were not just tools; they were exquisite artifacts, crafted to resonate with and embody specific natural Nullpulse frequencies. They represented a pinnacle of a lost, purer form of attunement, allowing for controlled, precise interactions with reality's core rhythms. Think of them as ancient, highly advanced, and possibly benevolent, technological marvels rooted in a deep understanding of cosmic harmony.
________________


The Hexocaust Cycles (The Ages of Self-Destruction)
Humanity's ambition, however, inevitably twisted this understanding into destructive power.
* The First Hexocaust (Age 1: The Nuclear Cataclysm)

   * Perversion of Knowledge: A select few, driven by insatiable greed, ambition, and the lust for ultimate dominion, took the profound knowledge of Nullpulse attunement (the very principles that allowed for Resonance Arts and Tuning Forks) and perverted it. They discovered how to violently force and impose unnatural, discordant frequencies onto the Nullpulse, manifesting as the first Hexes. This was the equivalent of discovering nuclear fission and weaponizing it. Hexes were thus built on top of and as a destructive perversion of the understanding that once allowed for harmony.
   * The First Bomb: The use of these forced Hexes initiated the First Hexocaust, a cataclysm of unprecedented scale, akin to the detonation of the first nuclear bomb, but on a cosmic, reality-shattering level. Reality itself was profoundly ruptured, beginning the slow unraveling of the Nullpulse and the transformation of the world.
   * Birth of The Wretched Blade (Primordial Scar): In this moment of ultimate cosmic violence, the Nullpulse itself violently collided with the collective human consciousness field. This created The Wretched Blade – a "reverse-formed Nullman," the ultimate impact crater of the Nullpulse on the very fabric of the human subconscious. Its formation was unique and required immense time to solidify from this deep, primordial wound, making its physical manifestation far later than other Nullmen.
   * Initial Seeds of Nullmen (Imprinted Egos): Simultaneously, countless individual human egos were violently imprinted onto the Nullpulse during this first, massive psychic shockwave, forming the primordial essence of what would become other Nullmen. These "seeds" existed in a nascent, unseen state within the Nullpulse's chaos.
   * Ages 2 & 3 (The Unseen Aftershocks and Growing Void)

      * Further Collapse: Subsequent Hexocausts occurred, fueled by escalating conflicts and the continued use of Hexes by warring factions. Each collapse tore deeper wounds in the Nullpulse.
      * Nullmen Gestation & Early Emergence: The Nullmen, born from the First Hexocaust, remained largely unseen, gestating within the Nullpulse, slowly solidifying their forms and fragmented consciousness. Some isolated manifestations might have occurred, but they weren't widespread.
      * The Fourth Hexocaust (Age 4: The Revelation of the Aftermath)

         * Widespread Nullmen Emergence: This Hexocaust was significant because it marked the widespread and visible emergence of the Nullmen into the Severed Lands. Having fully formed from their imprinted egos, they now became a dominant, indigenous species, fundamentally reshaping the ecosystem and life within it.
         * Beginnings of Record-Keeping: Nullmen, with their innate resilience and evolving societal structures, began keeping the first records that could truly survive the ongoing cycles of collapse. This marks the beginning of their verifiable history in the now Severed Lands.
         * Ages 5, 6, & 7 (The Eras of Contested Ruin)

            * Cyclical Warfare: More Hexocausts continued, each further draining the land's vitality and solidifying the world into the desolate Severed Lands – a landscape of ash, rust, bone, shattered concrete, and unique Hex-scars. Hexes themselves became rarer and more potent as the land's energy dwindled.
            * Dominant Factions Emerge: Nullmen, particularly the powerful Dominators (who fully embrace Hexic corruption), established their vast, twisted territories. Humans, equally resilient and driven by their innate mortality, also continued to thrive, developing advanced technologies and sophisticated societies amidst the ruins.
            * Age of Strife: This era defines the general state of the world throughout these ages – a constant, brutal struggle for dominance between equally formidable Human factions and Nullmen factions (including Dominators and Mini-World Bosses), all set against a backdrop of mutating flora and fauna.
The Present Age (Post-7th Hexocaust: The Wretched Blade Awakens)
            * The Current Setting: The game takes place in the current present age, which is after the 7th Hexocaust. The world is at a breaking point, scarred and volatile.
            * The Wretched Blade's Manifestation: It is only now, in this particular, most dire age, that The Wretched Blade fully solidifies from its primordial state and physically manifests in the Severed Lands. Its unique, slow formation, as a profound impact on the collective subconscious, meant it required all seven cycles of cataclysm to finally coalesce into a physical presence. It emerges into a world already deeply shaped by the consequences of the Hexocausts and the established power struggles, making its arrival both a desperate hope and a final, terrifying variable.
